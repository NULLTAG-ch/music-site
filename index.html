„Kino ohne Bilder. Filme entstehen im Kopf, nicht auf der Leinwand. Cinematic techno · cosmic frenchcore · German underground."

NULLTAG is a one-person Swiss music label. Releases ship across several Schienen — release rails sharing a sound profile and a visual signature. Catalog numbers, BPM, key, and ISRC are treated as design, not metadata to hide.

Brutalist verbal language, single TIEFROT mark per surface, JetBrains Mono everywhere. A small operation that happens to spec releases like a research lab — physics-as-lyrics, BPM-as-tag.
